A typical docking preparation

  1. Fetch the target, for example 1M17 (EGFR kinase with erlotinib), and check the resolution in the overview.
  2. In Select & export, keep chain A and the co-crystallised ligand, and leave the waters and buffer molecules out.
  3. Download the protein on its own as the receptor and the ligand as SDF for redocking. The ligand centre is shown next to it for your search box.
  4. Use the sequence panel to find binding-site residues. Clicking a residue shows it in 3D.

HoloSift is part of the CADD Toolkit of The Insilico Lab. It runs entirely in your browser, and files you open are not uploaded anywhere. Structure data and annotations come from the RCSB Protein Data Bank; 3D graphics use 3Dmol.js.
